# Community conduct

Discuss the work and treat people with respect. Technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment, discriminatory attacks, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing and spam are not.

Use reproducible examples, explain tradeoffs and critique code rather than people. Respect contributors' time and privacy. Do not post private media or personal details without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions and limit participation. Decisions should reflect the behavior and its impact, with room to correct an honest mistake.

For abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's report/block tools. For project moderation, contact a maintainer through an available private channel. Do not publish sensitive details while requesting contact.
